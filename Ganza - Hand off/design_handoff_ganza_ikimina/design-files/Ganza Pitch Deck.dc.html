01 Problem statement
Millions of Rwandans already save together. The record is the weak link.
Recording errors
“Sometimes the person writing can make a mistake and record less.”
Limited visibility
Members call a leader or wait for the next meeting to know their position.
Admin burden
Treasurers collect, total, record expenses and reconcile deposits by hand.
Invisible history
Years of saving stay in a notebook instead of becoming usable proof.
Who is affected Members · Treasurers · Presidents · Secretaries
02 Innovation & differentiation
We never hold the group’s money.
Members keep using the bank, SACCO, Mobile Money or cash system they already trust. A wallet asks “should I trust this platform with my money?” — Ganza removes that decision.
The ritual stays
Meet → Contribute → Discuss → Decide → Support
The friction goes
Counting → Totalling → Reconciling → Chasing → Explaining

07 Business model & what comes next
No percentage on withdrawals.
Charging people whenever they reach their own savings creates friction. A flat fee keeps the notebook honest.
Business model
~100 RWF per member
Simple, predictable and transparent — the group knows the cost before it joins.
Future · payment intelligence
Today: Record → Deposit → Human confirmation
Next: Record → Deposit → eKash confirmation → Automatic reconciliation
Not only what the treasurer recorded, but when the money actually arrived.
The Ganza difference
We are not asking Rwandans to trust a new place with their money — we are making the place they already trust easier to record and verify.
