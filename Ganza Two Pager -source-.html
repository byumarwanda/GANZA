01 Problem statement
Rwandans already save together. The writing down is what breaks.
Ibimina run on trust and meetings. The money is handled well. The writing down is not.
Recording errors
“Sometimes the person writing can make a mistake and record less.” One wrong line is hard to undo.
Limited visibility
To know what they have saved, members must call a leader or wait for the next meeting.
Administrative burden
The treasurer counts, adds up, writes expenses and checks the deposit — all by hand.
Invisible history
Years of saving stay in a notebook. No bank will ever see them.
Who is affected Members · Treasurers · Presidents · Secretaries
02 Innovation & differentiation
We never hold the group’s money.
The group keeps using the bank, SACCO, Mobile Money or cash it already uses. A wallet makes people ask: should I trust this app with my savings? Ganza never asks that question. That is why groups can start today.
The ritual stays Meet → Contribute → Discuss → Decide The friction goes Counting → Totalling → Reconciling

04 Payment process flow
Ganza never touches the money. It records who paid, what was spent, and who approved.
Member
Contributes money
→
Treasurer
Records the contribution
→
Ganza
Balances + SMS receipt
→
Meeting
Expenses recorded
→
Ganza
Net deposit amount
→
Treasurer
Banks it the group’s usual way
→
President / Secretary
Approves it. Now it is final.
Exception built in
If the treasurer is away, the president or secretary can stand in. They still cannot approve their own deposit — the other leader does.
Where money actually moves
Through the group’s own bank, SACCO, Mobile Money or cash. Ganza only records the amount, the receipt and who approved it.

Business model
A flat fee per member. Never a cut of savings.
Taking a cut when someone touches their own savings is the fastest way to lose trust. We charge a flat fee for each member instead. What that fee should be is the most sensitive question we have, and we are asking real ibimina before we answer it.
Future · payment intelligence
Today: Record → Deposit → Human confirmation
Next: Record → Deposit → eKash confirmation → Automatic reconciliation
Then Ganza knows not only what was written, but when the money truly landed.
We are not asking anyone to move their money. We are making the place they already trust easier to write down, and easier to check.
